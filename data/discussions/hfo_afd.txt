Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service Honolulu HI
323 AM HST Sat Oct 10 2026

.SYNOPSIS...

Moisture will decrease through the weekend with light to moderate
trades returning. Showers will impact windward and mauka
locations. A brief increase in showers expected Monday night on 
the Big Island before a return to relatively dry trades through 
the end of next week.

&&

.DISCUSSION...

Currently at 3 AM HST, satellite and radar imagery showed 
scattered clouds and showers moving along mostly windward and 
mauka locations across the state. 

The upper level low that brought rain to the area this week will 
weaken and move away to the northeast over the next few days. A 
surface high pressure system to our northeast will strengthen and 
bring easterly trades back to the region. Apart from a brief 
increase in moisture mainly for the Big Island Monday, the next 
several days will be dominated by relatively dry trades. Isolated 
to scattered showers windward and mauka will be most frequent at 
night. Otherwise, expect light to moderate winds and rather 
typical October conditions.

&&

.AVIATION...

Moderate trade winds build back into the islands through the 
weekend. VFR conditions will prevail at all airfields.

No AIRMETS are currently in effect or expected.

&&

.MARINE...

Moderate to fresh trade winds will build back over the majority 
of the coastal waters this weekend, though weaker winds will 
remain in place over the western waters as a trough continues to 
linger west of the area. Although there will be slight day to day 
fluctuations, gentle to fresh trades will prevail throughout much 
of next week.

The current moderate, long period south swell will fade gradually
through the weekend, but a long-period reinforcement is set to 
arrive on Saturday, then peak Sunday into Monday. This will 
maintain elevated surf along south and west- facing shores, but it
will remain below advisory levels. South shore surf will decline 
slightly by the middle of next week, but will be followed by a new
long period swell Wednesday night or Thursday.

A medium-period northwest swell declines through the weekend as 
the source low pressure northwest of the Hawaiian Islands 
continues to weaken through Monday. This will cause small surf 
along north-facing shores to begin to fade today. A smaller long- 
period northwest swell is also forecast to move through the 
islands this weekend, which could prolong small surf through 
Sunday, but will fade by the beginning of next week. A small, 
long-period northwest swell is forecast to arrive on Monday and 
peak Tuesday, boosting north shore surf back to moderate levels.

Surf along east-facing shores will remain small as local winds 
remain fairly weak with only slight variations each day. Small, 
moderate-period west and east swells generated by multiple Pacific
tropical cyclones are also moving through the islands this 
weekend.


&&

.FIRE WEATHER...

Light to moderate trades expected. Moisture will decrease 
Saturday night into Sunday, bringing RH values down to near 
dangerous fire conditions Monday on part of the Big Island, 
including near Kona. Inversion heights are around 5500-6500 
feet.
